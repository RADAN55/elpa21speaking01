SCHOOL LIFE • LANGUAGE LAB
25 original ELPA21-style activities • Grades 9–12
UPDATED: word-choice writing, speaking recordings, and teacher collection

PUBLISH ON YOUR EXISTING GITHUB SITE
1. Unzip this package.
2. In the elpa21speaking01 repository, choose Add file > Upload files.
3. Upload BOTH index.html and teacher.html into the same folder used by
   your published workbook. Replace the old index.html. Commit the change.
4. Open your published workbook after deployment finishes and refresh.
   The header should say UPDATED EDITION • WRITING + RECORDING + SUBMISSIONS.
5. Open Teacher collection dashboard from the workbook sidebar.
Do not upload the ZIP as your website. GitHub must receive the extracted files.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- All 25 existing reading, listening, speaking, and extended writing tasks.
- Three original sentence dropdowns in each Writing section: 75 in total.
- Word-choice feedback and explanations; students may revise and retry.
- Record, Stop, playback, and audio download controls in each Speaking section.
- Optional Vocaroo button and a field for the student's recording link.
- Per-activity submission downloads with answers and the actual recorded audio.
- Teacher dashboard: import files, review all answers, play audio, write feedback,
  mark reviewed, export class CSV, and save/reopen a class review with audio.

TEACHER: SET UP COLLECTION
This is a static website. It does not have accounts or a server inbox.
1. Make a file-upload assignment in your existing classroom platform (LMS).
   The assignment must allow .json attachments. Each activity submission
   contains both the answers and the student's in-page recording.
2. Open the teacher dashboard and expand Set up your student submission link.
3. Enter the published workbook URL and your assignment's upload-page URL.
4. Press Make student link. Share the resulting link with your students.
   Their workbook will now show Open teacher upload page in every activity.
5. Students download their activity submission, open your assignment, and
   attach the file there. Downloading alone does NOT submit anything.
6. Download those attachments from your assignment. In the teacher dashboard,
   select multiple student files to import them together.
7. Open each row to hear the recording and review writing and other responses.
8. Download class CSV for a spreadsheet of results and feedback. Save class
   review to preserve imported recordings and feedback for another session.
9. Reopen a saved class review through the same file picker.

If your platform blocks .json files, have students submit the text response
report and separate audio download or Vocaroo link through that platform.
Those separate files cannot be imported into the collection dashboard as a
single submission; review them in your LMS instead.

STUDENT ROUTINE
1. Enter your name and class before recording.
2. Complete Reading, Listening, Speaking, and Writing, including word choices.
3. Press Record, allow your microphone, speak, press Stop, and listen back.
   Recording stops after three minutes (a classroom limit, not ELPA21 timing).
4. Or, with your teacher's permission, open Vocaroo, record there, and paste
   the share link into the Vocaroo field. It opens in a separate tab.
5. At the bottom, press Download activity submission.
6. Upload that .json file to your teacher's assignment. The workbook does not
   automatically send it or verify that the teacher has received it.

SAVING AND AUDIO
- Text responses are saved in this browser when storage is available.
- In-page recordings stay only while this page is open. Download your activity
  submission or audio before refreshing or leaving. Recording again replaces
  the previous recording for that activity after confirmation.
- Download my work is a text-only backup. It does not include microphone audio.
- A submission file includes only ONE activity, its responses, and its audio.
- The teacher dashboard keeps its session in memory. Save class review before
  leaving; the CSV alone does not contain playable audio.
- Use Start a new student after downloading work when sharing a device.

MICROPHONE TROUBLESHOOTING
Use the published HTTPS workbook. Allow microphone access and check the device
microphone. Browser, device, and school policies may restrict recording.
If recording is unavailable, use teacher-approved Vocaroo or another school
recording tool. Students can submit writing even without a recording; both
student and teacher pages show when a recording is missing.
Audio is stored in the format supported by the student's browser (such as
WebM, MP4/M4A, or Ogg). If playback fails, download the audio and try a compatible
player. Browser microphone capture was tested with simulated media APIs during
construction; a real microphone on your school devices has not been tested.

PRACTICE, NOT OFFICIAL TEST MATERIAL
All questions are original ELPA21-style classroom practice. This workbook does
not duplicate official ELPA21 test items or certify exact test appearance.
Scores are current, retryable practice checks, not official proficiency levels.
Student files can be edited, so they are not secure assessment records.
Speaking and longer writing require teacher feedback.
Framework: https://elpa21.org/elp-standards/
Assessment overview: https://elpa21.org/summative/
Recording API: https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/MediaRecorder

STUDENT INFORMATION
Keep submissions in your teacher-managed classroom platform. Do not upload
student answers, names, or recordings to the public GitHub repository.
The workbook makes no automatic upload. Vocaroo is a separate service; opening
and using it stores recordings according to that service's own operation.
